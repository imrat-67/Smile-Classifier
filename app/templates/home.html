Smile Classifier

A binary image classifier that detects whether a face is smiling or not.

Approach

  • Framework: scikit-learn
  • Model: SVM (linear kernel)
  • Preprocessing: OpenCV — grayscale, resized to 64x64, flattened to a feature vector
  • Why: Fast to train, lightweight, and effective on small datasets
